Who it fits

Sprint fits if all four are already true:

  • You are already selling.
  • You can name the audience.
  • You have a price.
  • You already have traffic or subscribers.

You choose one track. Track A is one landing page. Track B is up to three emails.

Sprint does not fit if you need the audience found, the price set, or the demand built. It also does not fit if you need a call, ads, a multi-page site, a retainer, or a results guarantee.

What the brief needs

The clock does not start until the brief is complete. Send:

  • What you sell, and the price.
  • Who it is for.
  • Where those people already come from (the site, the list, or another source you already have).
  • The track. For emails, the job of each one (up to three).
  • Tone, plus any line that must stay or must not be said.
  • Facts you want used. If you have none, say so. We do not invent proof.

We do not research the market, and we do not set the offer.

FAQ

No. Sprint is async. There is no call in this pack.

When the brief is complete, not on the day you pay if the brief is still missing pieces.
What if I need a page and emails?
That is two tracks. One seat is one track.
Can I have more than one revision?
Not in this pack. Extra rounds are quoted separately.
