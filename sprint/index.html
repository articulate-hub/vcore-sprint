FAQ

Is there a strategy call?
No. Sprint is async. Custom work can include calls; this pack does not.
When does the 7 business days start?
When your intake brief is complete — not on payment day if the brief is still partial.
What if I need both a landing page and emails?
That is outside a single Sprint seat. Ask for a custom quote, or buy separate seats in separate months if capacity allows (still one track per pack).
Can I get more revisions?
Not inside the pack. Extra rounds are quoted separately.
What if I don’t like the copy?
Taste alone is not a refund ground. Refunds apply only if work is undelivered or we breach the stated SLA (7 business days from a complete brief, one revision round as defined).
Who is this not for?
Anyone needing brand strategy, multi-page sites, ads, retainers, unlimited revisions, or guaranteed ROI.
Where does payment go?
Stripe Checkout on Vcore’s account (owner: Lale). Acquisition partners do not take payment.
